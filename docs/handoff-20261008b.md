| Job (one DSP job each) | vs stock | DSP time |

Both halves, all generator blocks and the tail run on the DSP; har (harmonic source STFT) is still captured.
Design choices to keep: 16-bit activations as two byte planes, W8x2 weights, three-group HMX plane convs
(low x low group read through its low plane, per-channel range 2^g), Q22 phase turns, per-channel residual
scales (covering conv2 peaks), per-input-channel conv scales for tiny Snake alpha, transposed convs as
polyphase HMX convs + HVX phase-pair interleave, the iSTFT folded into one HMX conv, fine/coarse magnitude
windows in the tail. Every fixture builder checks its index maps against stock floats (130+ dB).

## Next steps, in order

1. Chain the halves in one job: the 10x section's mean -> LeakyReLU(0.1) (`Kokoro.LeakyRelu16.ps1 -Slope 0.1`)
   -> the 60x front's ups[1] input planes (today they come from the input buffer); the 60x front fixture must
   then take the 10x stage's output scales as its ups[1] input scales. Both sections in one step list needs
   the stage emitter split into a per-section builder (bodies named per channel count; one worker pool).
2. Harmonic source + STFT on the DSP (`SineGen`: f0 x300 nearest upsample, nine harmonics, captured random
   phases and noise, linear 9 -> 1, tanh; STFT n_fft 20 hop 5 with atan2 phase), writing har in both layouts
   (60x: 22 channels; 10x: phase-major 132 channels).
3. Then speed (instruction packing only after studying the V73 PRM packet rules and an end-to-end receipt),
   the decoder (W4 weights proposed), predictors, text encoder, ALBERT; the phonemizer joins at token IDs.
