# Handoff, 2026-10-10

Supersedes `docs/handoff-20261009d.md` for direction. Start with `pwsh -NoProfile -File ./Invoke-KokoroDevelopment.ps1`.

## What changed

Stock Kokoro's decoder now speaks held-out sentences from a front end with no neural network: the compiled phonemizer,
`t_en` units sampled from a bank of stock captures, and durations, N and F0 predicted by PowerShell-learned tables from
an aligned linguistic stream. ALBERT, the five predictor BiLSTMs and the text encoder run only offline, to fill the
bank. Listening: "different, not worse". Receipts, in order:

1. `docs/results/prosody-substitution-zira-20261010.md`: Zira metadata does not replace the predictor. PCM SNR
   across different noise draws is about 19 dB.
2. `docs/results/unit-selection-smoke-20261010.md`: bank-assembled decoder inputs are bit-exact with stock.
3. `docs/results/unit-bank-curve-20261010.md`: 43,679-sentence bank (2.43M units, int16 `t_en` transparent).
   Sampled `t_en` converges to 1.17 against a 0.64 floor. Per-unit F0/N does not improve with bank size, and phrase N
   transplant fails.
4. `docs/results/substitution-refinement-20261010.md`: 1,140 decoder-verified substitutions. The symbol two to the
   right (R2) predicts the effect. Right-weighted `TargetCost` is lowered with exact parity.
5. `docs/results/prosody-two-stream-20261010.md`: N is the output log-energy envelope (r = 0.988). Two values per
   token reach 2.64 (ceiling). The table-predicted front end reaches 4.68, against 4.90 for unit-sampled prosody.

This front end makes a Kokoro variant. Phase-1 stock benchmarks still need the stock front end (AGENTS.md "Model
changes"). Benchmarks of this path are labeled as the variant.

## Pitfalls found

- PowerShell `-eq`/`-ne` on `[char]` ignore case. Phoneme symbols are case-significant (A/a, I/i, O/o, W/w, Y/y):
  use `-ceq`/`-cne` and Ordinal comparers. The pinned compiler compiles `[char] -ne` ordinally, a parity gap to
  report to PwshCompiler.
- The comma binds tighter than `+` and `-`: parenthesize index arithmetic.
- PSPerception `Invoke-PerceptRefine` admits by training contradictions (it chose features that do not generalize),
  and `Refine.ps1:460` assumes `SpecimenName`. Both are for the PSPerception repository.
- The driver resolves clause grammar for about 1 in 2,000 sentences, and the Moby capability table omits P, C and v.
  The experiments read Moby tags directly.

## Next

1. Lower the whole selector into a CoreLib-only assembly: an indexed bank (packed context keys, CSR candidate arrays),
   the token-feature tables, the Viterbi, and the gather of `t_en`, durations, N and F0. Spend storage to remove
   computation: keep the bank large and int16 (proven transparent), precompute keys, rankings, transition penalties
   and backoff decisions, and memory-map the bank with hot contexts resident. No runtime decompression. The output
   is the decoder job's 16-bit `asr`/F0/N layout. The acceptance criteria are intelligibility and naturalness at
   least equal to the table baseline, warm time to first audio, and bounded CPU work per utterance. Parity with
   `tools/reference/unit-bank/mine.ps1` and `Measure-ProsodyFeatures.ps1`. new command `UnitBank`.
2. Chunked decoding with supplied AdaIN statistics. Supplied statistics are part of chunking, not a later refinement:
   on Windows, three slices with their own statistics reach 3.0 dB waveform SNR against one pass, and with
   whole-utterance statistics 32.3 dB at 400 ms overlap (26.4 dB at 100 ms) (`docs/speed-target.md`, 2026-10-09).
   The DSP moments record is additive (int32 S1, A2, AB, B2 per channel; `src/kernels/Kokoro.AdaInMoments16.ps1`), and
   the coefficient step takes the frame count in r3 (`src/jobs/Kokoro.DecoderRun16.ps1:112-116`). The supplied mode
   replaces the zero fill and the moments call with a DMA of a per-site record plus its whole-utterance frame count.
   Generator sites over long utterances exceed the int32 bound (stated for 7,801 frames), so their supplied records
   need wider sums or a pre-shift. Steps: (a) the ceiling, whole-utterance records from a stock capture injected into
   chunks on SM8550; (b) records from the bank, per-unit partial sums at each site from donor renders, summed over the
   selected units, measured against (a); (c) cut at N minima within 96 frames. The harmonic source draws its own
   randomness on the DSP and carries phase and noise state between chunks (today the draws come from the capture).
   Then phonemizer -> selector -> DSP job -> AAudio, with RTF and time-to-first-audio receipts on SM8550, then SM8635,
   labeled as the network-free variant. new command `Speak`.
3. Pitch controller: state, lookahead window and per-token slope as a damped command-response controller. Score
   against the 2.64 ceiling, by listening, and with an offline intelligibility check. Score with `Status`.
