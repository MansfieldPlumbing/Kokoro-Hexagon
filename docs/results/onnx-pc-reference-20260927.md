## Observed result

`tools/reference/Invoke-KokoroOnnxReference.ps1` produced 48 PCM files and matching playable WAV files from 24 deterministic cases, with two runs per case. All 48 outputs had finite samples, valid lengths, and peak magnitude below 1.0. The two runs of every case were byte-identical, and a separate fresh-process rerun of the first case was also byte-identical.

The case lengths span 7–94 phoneme characters and produced 1.400–6.425 seconds of audio. Second-run CPU inference time spanned 450.5–3560.6 ms, with a 1661.0 ms median; audio duration divided by that inference time spanned 1.69–3.19. These are PC ONNX reference timings, not Hexagon or product-path measurements. The ignored full receipt is `build/onnx-reference-24/receipt.json`.

## Evidence boundary

The exported ONNX graph is a differential wayfinder, not the original checkpoint/source contract or a release input. The style rows match the pinned stock voice exactly, but graph weights and numerical parity against the original checkpoint have not been established here. No direct Hexagon whole-model output exists yet for same-input waveform comparison, and no QNN/device result was mixed into this PC reference set. An FFT-based mutation objective must remain gated until a candidate output can be compared against this explicitly labeled ONNX reference and the relevant stock operator boundaries.
