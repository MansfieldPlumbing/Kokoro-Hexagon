## SM8550

3/3 runs passed (`tools/Invoke-GeneratorTailProbe.ps1`), every run's output identical, and the phone's output equal to the
V73 simulator's byte for byte (163,840 bytes). Median DSP time 14.445 ms (277,349 ticks at 19.2 MHz; 26-30 ms per invoke
including dispatch), one HVX thread, weight DMA not overlapped with HMX. Not yet measured: the decoder joined to the
generator job, speed work, SM8635.

## Found on the way

- An HMX `:single` activation read whose two croutons (Rs and Rs + dY) lie in different 1 MiB regions faults in the V73
  simulator (exception 0x26); the layout keeps HMX-read regions inside one 1 MiB page
  (`docs/generator60x-resident-design.md`, correction).
- Folding per-channel input scales up to 27x apart into one W8 row starved most columns (encode 30 dB); 1x1 convs now read
  identity windows in one common scale.
- The pool's per-channel gains are Q15 (`q31(a 2^16, W) = a W / 2^15`); the first fixture wrote Q31 and saturated decode.3.
