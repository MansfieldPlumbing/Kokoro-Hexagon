## Phones

Skel `src/emit/Kokoro.DmaBenchProbe.ps1` (`libkokoro_dma_bench_skel.so`, SHA-256
`7591B3CC3B828125C0EE13FBE8F81C694A0AE56FED695A32F84EEA421869E90F`, bytes match the
pinned assembler), run by `tools/Invoke-DmaBenchProbe.ps1`. Each invocation fills the input
with fresh random bytes, DMAs it into VTCM, checks VTCM against the input word by word,
DMAs VTCM to an output region that only DMA writes, and the host compares that region by
SHA-256. Immediately after each `dmwait` the skel reads both descriptors' done bits and the
last copied word. Then 8 timed repetitions of each copy. Ticks at 19.2 MHz.

| SoC | Runs passed | Checked DMA in (ms) | Checked DMA out (ms) | DMA in, 8 reps (ms each) | DMA out, 8 reps (ms each) | memcpy in (ms) | memcpy out (ms) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| SM8550 | 3/3 | 0.040–0.041 | 0.054–0.055 | 0.056–0.075 | 0.053 | 2.36–2.56 | 8.21 |
| SM8635 | 3/3 | 0.037–0.038 | 0.040–0.043 | 0.041–0.042 | 0.038–0.040 | 1.69–1.75 | 8.21 |

Every run: done bits 3 (both descriptors) and last-word XOR 0 right after `dmwait`;
VTCM check 0; DMA-only output region equal to the input; release 0. VTCM granted:
8,388,608 B (SM8550), 4,194,304 B (SM8635).

For one 1,998,848 B tensor, DMA is 37–55 GB/s; memcpy is 0.8–1.2 GB/s into VTCM and
0.24 GB/s out of VTCM. The out figure matches the frozen worker's profile (147.8 ms
for 35,979,264 B of VTCM→DDR copies, 0.243 GB/s).

Limits of this measurement: the source was written by the host just before the call and may
sit in the system cache, so these rates are not DDR-only bandwidth. One tensor size, one
descriptor pair, no overlap with compute.
